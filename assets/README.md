# Icon asset

`obsidian.svg` preserves the geometry of Obsidian's official simplified
favicon from <https://obsidian.md/favicon.svg>. Its three facets are converted
to white so `ObsidianIcon.qml` can use the mark as a monochrome mask and tint
it with the active Omarchy theme at runtime.

Obsidian states that its icon is designed to remain legible in single-color
applications and may be personalized for personal use. Obsidian and its logo
are trademarks of Dynalist Inc. This community plugin is not affiliated with
or endorsed by Dynalist Inc.
